Privacy Policy

YakuBus (yakushimabus.com) provides independent travel and public transport information for Yakushima. This policy explains how the website and its operator use data.

Website analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and improve the site. Reports may include pages viewed, referral source, approximate location, device and browser information, and interactions such as selecting bus stops, looking up fares or clicking an external link. We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with pages, including through heatmaps and session recordings. These services may use cookies or similar technologies.

Advertising, affiliate and booking links. The site includes Google advertising code. When ads are served, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Some links lead to booking partners such as Klook and Viator. We may earn a commission from eligible bookings. If you leave YakuBus to book, the provider handles the booking and payment under its own privacy policy.

English. If you subscribe, we use your email address and consent record to send occasional Yakushima transport information, practical travel advice, island discoveries and related service recommendations. Campaign emails are currently sent in English. Brevo processes these details and manages confirmation, delivery and unsubscribe requests on our behalf. Brevo may also process necessary data for platform security; see Brevo’s Privacy Policy. Complete your subscription using the link in the confirmation email. You can unsubscribe through any newsletter or contact the operator below to request deletion of your subscription data. Our website stores only a local submission marker to hide repeat invitations in this browser; it does not store your email address in browser storage or send it to our analytics. This marker does not verify email confirmation and cannot recognize you on other devices. The footer’s Get travel emails link opens the sign-up page.
