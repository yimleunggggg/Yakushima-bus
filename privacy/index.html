Privacy Policy

YakuBus (yakushimabus.com) provides independent travel and public transport information for Yakushima. This policy explains how the website and its operator use data.

Website analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and improve the site. Reports may include pages viewed, referral source, approximate location, device and browser information, and interactions such as selecting bus stops, looking up fares or clicking an external link. We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with pages, including through heatmaps and session recordings. These services may use cookies or similar technologies.

Advertising and booking links. The site includes Google advertising code. When ads are served, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Some links lead to booking partners such as Klook and Viator. We may earn a commission from eligible bookings. If you leave YakuBus to book, the provider handles the booking and payment under its own privacy policy.

Search Console authorization. The site operator authorizes a separate reporting tool to read Search Console performance data for this website. It uses Google's read-only Search Console permission to retrieve reports such as search queries, clicks and impressions for site maintenance. Visitors do not need to sign in with Google or grant this permission to use YakuBus. The operator's authorization credentials are used to access Search Console and are not part of the public website.

Your choices. You can manage cookies in your browser. See Google's partner sites privacy information, Google Ad Settings and the Microsoft Privacy Statement for provider details and choices.

Contact. For questions about this policy or the use of your information, contact the YakuBus operator.
